Privacy Policy & Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Qalat ("the app") is an offline Tigrinya ↔ English dictionary published by Ammaniel Hintza LLC.

Data we collect

None. Qalat does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network requests. All dictionary data is bundled inside the app.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs.
  • Saved words, recent history, and settings are stored only on your device (local storage) and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Qalat collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third-party data

Dictionary content is bundled from openly licensed sources (MIT, CC0, CC BY-SA 4.0); full attribution is available in the app under Settings → Data sources & licenses. No third-party services receive any user data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Ammaniel Hintza LLC — ammaniel.hintza@googlemail.com

Support

Questions, bug reports, or word corrections: email ammaniel.hintza@googlemail.com with the word and the issue. We review corrections against the source dictionaries and ship them in data updates.

About Qalat

Qalat (ቃል — "word") is a clean, fast, fully offline Tigrinya ↔ English dictionary with 17,448 entries merged from four open dictionary sources. Companion to GeezEasy.
